## 1. Distribution mechanics as reported by communities: view plateaus, shadowbans, first hour, frequency, reposting, deleting, Trial Reels, warm-up, pods, bought followers, automation

### Takeaway
The "200-view jail" is a real, commonly observed plateau but there is no evidence of a formal cap; the only verifiable restriction is recommendation ineligibility shown in Account Status, which since April 2026 also hits accounts that mostly repost others' work. Most "rules" circulating in communities (first hour decides everything, deleting hurts, editing a caption hurts) have no primary source behind them.

### Cited Findings
**View plateau ("200-view jail")**
- [ANECDOTE] A BlackHatWorld thread reports Reels stalling around 200 views on two new accounts despite separate device/SIM and residential proxy; a reply says to post for at least two weeks to a month before calling it "jail", because older accounts gain trust. No verified fix is given — [BlackHatWorld](https://www.blackhatworld.com/posts/20746186/)
- [PRACTITIONER] The plateau is described as not an official TikTok policy or formal restriction, but the result of an initial small-audience test — [Multilogin](https://multilogin.com/?p=111497)
- [PRACTITIONER] A music-marketing piece states no reliable universal rule guarantees the same initial test pool or threshold for every platform or account (attribution from search summary; likely this page) — [Jack Righteous](https://jackrighteous.com/ja/blogs/ai-music-distribution-guide/break-the-200-view-barrier-maximize-engagement-for-music-creators)
- [OFFICIAL, via vendor blog] Instagram does not publish a "trust score"; warm-up timelines are heuristics from marketing blogs and forums — [Superscale](https://superscale.ai/learn/the-right-way-to-warm-up-new-tiktok-instagram-accounts/)

**Shadowban vs real restrictions**
- [OFFICIAL, second-hand] Account Status shows whether an account/content is eligible to be recommended to non-followers (Explore, Reels, suggested accounts), flags "ineligible" content and offers an appeal — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/shadowbanned/); [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-shadowban/)
- [OFFICIAL, second-hand, date disputed] Mosseri is quoted saying "in connected ranking, we do not limit reach"; one blog dates it February 2025, another February 2026. Not resolved — [ContentStudio](https://contentstudio.io/blog/instagram-shadowban); [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-shadowban/)
- [ANECDOTE] Claims that eligibility can be removed with no recorded violation, and that a healthy account gets 20-40% of Reels reach from non-followers, have no primary source — [Unfollr](https://www.unfollr.com/blog/instagram-shadowban)
- [OFFICIAL] 30 April 2026: Instagram excludes accounts whose posts are mostly unoriginal from recommendation surfaces (extended from Reels to photos and carousels). Mosseri: such an account is "no longer going to be recommendable". Followers still see the posts; status is visible and appealable in Account Status — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026-04-30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)

**First hour**
- [OFFICIAL, second-hand] In a Mosseri Q&A as summarised by Kontentino: strong correlation between first-hour and overall performance, but no guarantee either way; a slow start does not mean failure — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/does-early-engagement-determine-how-well-an-instagram-post-will-perform-overall/)
- [MYTH, not supported] RecurPost reports that no primary Instagram/Meta document it reviewed supports "first-hour engagement determines total reach" — [RecurPost](https://blog.recurpost.com/?p=22)
- [DATA STUDY] Metricool 2026 (24M+ posts, 375,118 accounts): 65% of a Reel's views and 61% of its interactions arrive in the first three days — [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/etude-instagram-formats-leviers-fonctionnent-2026/)

**Posting frequency**
- [DATA STUDY] Buffer (Aug 2025, 2.1M posts, 102K accounts; feed posts, Reels, carousels): 3-5 posts/week is called the sweet spot; secondary coverage reports about +12% reach per post at 3-5/week and +24% at 10/week versus 1-2/week — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/); [Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633)
- [DATA STUDY, conflicting] Other commentary on Buffer data says reach per individual post declines as frequency rises (more total reach, less per post), citing a 4.8M channel-week analysis of about 161,000 profiles — [Sociallyin](https://sociallyin.com/blog/can-you-post-too-much-on-instagram/)
- [DATA STUDY] Metricool YouTube 2026: channels posting 35+ Shorts a month saw long-form performance fall back to the level of channels posting none; suggested cadence 2-4 Shorts/week — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/)
- [ANECDOTE] Batch-posting several TikToks in a short window is said to hurt reach; not verified against Buffer's page — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/)

**Reposting, deleting, editing**
- [MYTH] Editing a caption after publishing does not hurt performance (Mosseri, second-hand) — [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/debunking-common-instagram-myths); [UNUM](https://www.unum.la/blog/adam-mosseri-shuts-down-instagram-engagement-myth-edits-do-boost-reach)
- [MYTH, not supported] No primary Instagram/Meta document states that deleting a post affects ranking or reach — [RecurPost](https://blog.recurpost.com/?p=22). One blogger claims mass-deleting hurts momentum (opinion only) — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-to-delete-reels-from-instagram-profile/)
- [OFFICIAL, second-hand] July 2026 AMA: deleting or archiving old Trial Reels is fine, since they never appear on the profile — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-trial-reels-archive-delete/)
- [MYTH] Sponsored/branded content is not down-ranked — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-says-does-not-reduce-the-reach-of-sponsored-content/732615/)

**Trial Reels**
- [OFFICIAL, Meta's own figures] 400,000+ creators reviewed: about 40% posted Reels more often after their first Trial Reel, and 80% of those increased non-follower reach. Scheduling of Trial Reels was added 2 April 2026. Collabs partners cannot be added — [Publer](https://publer.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels-guide/); [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-trial-reels-work-research.md)
- [PRACTITIONER, Reddit second-hand] Planoly's review of Reddit threads concludes results are "all over the place"; only a structured test on your own account is meaningful — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-trial-reels-work-research.md)
- [PRACTITIONER] Consensus method: one variable per trial, same measurement window (24h and 72h), treat it as a cold-audience test only — [Socialk.it](https://socialk.it/en/blog/instagram-trial-reels-strategy); [Postfa.st](https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels)

**Pods, bought followers, automation**
- [PRACTITIONER] Bought followers lower engagement rate; outright disablement is described as rare for a single purchase, more common when combined with bought comments or bot activity — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-bot-followers-work-instagram-why-avoid-them/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Pods are real people but create the same arranged signal; detection of pods specifically is unclear — [MakeInfluence](https://www.makeinfluence.com/en/academy/engagement-pods-a-different-red-flag-from-bought-followers)
- [ANECDOTE] ManyChat community threads report bans/disablement attributed to automation; ManyChat is an official Meta Business Partner using the Graph API — [ManyChat community](https://community.manychat.com/creator-conversations-72/manychat-got-my-instagram-account-banned-10265); [ManyChat community](https://community.manychat.com/general-q-a-43/instagram-business-account-disabled-due-to-automation-10161)

### Inferences
- Checkable QC items with strong support: Account Status shows no recommendation restriction; the account is not mostly reposted material; judge a Reel at 72 hours, not at one hour.
- Checkable but weakly supported: new accounts need two to four weeks of posting before a plateau means anything (forum heuristic only).
- Frequency: consensus that 3-5 posts/week is a safe floor; disagreement on whether per-post reach rises or falls beyond that. Do not use frequency as a pass/fail criterion.
- Deleting underperformers and editing captions are neutral according to available evidence; a checklist should not penalise either.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, Warrior Forum, IndieHackers, Hacker News, Quora or Discord material was reachable. Practitioner consensus on these questions is therefore not established here.
- No source found on reposting your own past winner (as opposed to others' content), on niche consistency versus variety, or on account warm-up beyond vendor heuristics.
- Exact thresholds of the April 2026 originality rule (share of reposts, time window) not retrieved.

## 2. Formats reported as over-performing, and how platforms treat AI, templated and unoriginal content

### Takeaway
Data studies agree on a split: Reels win reach, carousels win saves and engagement rate; episodic "show" formats are the main practitioner trend. Platforms say they do not penalise AI as such, but they demonetise or de-recommend mass-produced, repetitive and unoriginal content, and faceless channels report collateral damage on YouTube.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA STUDY] Buffer 2026 (52M+ posts, 2025 data): Reels get 36% more reach than carousels; carousels earn 12% more engagement — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement/)
- [DATA STUDY] Metricool 2026: carousels get more than double the reach and views of single images, nearly 4x interactions and 9x saves; Reel volume up 35%, carousels up 24% year on year — [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/etude-instagram-formats-leviers-fonctionnent-2026/); [Gens d'Internet](https://gensdinternet.fr/2026/07/03/les-carrousels-sur-instagram-en-2026-generent-9-fois-plus-denregistrements-que-les-photos/)
- [DATA STUDY] Metricool 2026: Reel engagement rate 4.30% (up 24.76%), about 1,035 interactions per Reel versus 237 per single image; Reels are the only format whose engagement rate grew — [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2026/07/05/cette-etude-revele-ce-qui-fonctionne-reellement-sur-instagram-en-2026/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/fr/statistiques-reels/)
- [DATA STUDY] Metricool short-video study 2026 (6,177,375 videos, 375,462 accounts, 90 days): TikTok averages 32,900 views per video, 42.6% more than Reels; TikTok leads on views for all tiers except 1M+ accounts, where Instagram leads; Shorts are lowest on every metric in every tier — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/short-video-report/); [Marketing4eCommerce](https://marketing4ecommerce.net/en/tiktok-instagram-or-youtube-which-platform-is-best-for-your-short-videos-study/)
- [DATA STUDY] TikTok saturation: Metricool (May 2026, 2.3M posts, 92,000 accounts) reports a 31% drop in views per video as volume rises; Socialinsider reports TikTok views down 23% year on year while brands posted 40% more — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/); [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-benchmarks/amp/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Rachel Karten's Link in Bio (titles and teasers only; paywalled): social "shows"/episodic series are not a passing trend (Dec 2025); "evidence of effort" (Mar 2026); Q1 2026 report lists delayed virality, voice-memo Reels, horizontal videos and a TikTok slowdown — [Link in Bio](https://www.milkkarten.net/p/brand-social-trend-report-q1-2026); [Link in Bio](https://www.milkkarten.net/p/evidence-of-effort)
- [PRACTITIONER] SocialPilot 2026: brands "think in episodes, not posts"; recurring character with real expertise cited as the design principle — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/de/blog/social-media-content-series-guide)
- [OFFICIAL] YouTube, effective 15 July 2025: "repetitious content" rule renamed "inauthentic content"; mass-produced or repetitive content is not monetisable; AI-assisted content remains eligible; described by YouTube's Rene Ritchie as a minor update — [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/07/10/youtube-is-fighting-ai-slop-with-new-monetization-guidelines/)
- [OFFICIAL, via press] January 2026: YouTube removed 16 AI "slop" channels; December 2025: Screen Culture and KH Studio terminated for AI fake trailers — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtubes-ai-slop-problem-and-how-marketers-can-compete/567297/)
- [PRACTITIONER, press] June 2026: faceless creators who never used AI report being caught by the crackdown — [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage)
- [OFFICIAL] Instagram, 31 Aug 2026: "AI-generated profile" label; unlabelled AI personas can lose reach; labelling carries no penalty. Mosseri says Instagram does not throttle content for being AI — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagrams-new-ai-label/); [Implicator](https://www.implicator.ai/instagram-will-cut-the-reach-of-ai-personas-that-skip-its-new-label.md)
- [OFFICIAL] TikTok, 18 Nov 2025: users can dial AI-generated content up or down in Manage Topics; invisible watermarking tested; 1.3 billion videos labelled. No data on creator reach impact — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/18/tiktok-now-lets-you-choose-how-much-ai-generated-content-you-want-to-see/)
- [OFFICIAL] Original means made by you or materially edited; adding a watermark, changing speed or reposting a screenshot with credit stays unoriginal. Meta says 75% of US recommendations are now original content — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/05/04/reposted-photos-and-carousels-now-face-reach-penalties-on-instagram/)
- [ANECDOTE] A page claiming a 15-80% reach penalty for the "AI info" label is unsourced — [ishort](https://ishort-pp.web.app/instagram-ai-content-label.html)

### Inferences
- Red flag for a faceless motion-design account: output that looks templated and interchangeable across episodes. The platforms' stated target is repetition and lack of human input, not animation or AI tooling.
- Format choice should follow purpose: Reel for non-follower reach, carousel for save-worthy reference content. This framing is Metricool's own recommendation and matches Buffer's numbers.
- Series formats have practitioner support but no performance data in these sources.

### Gaps
- No comparative data found for green screen, POV skits, "day in the life", listicles, storytime, ASMR or silent text-on-screen Reels.
- No measured effect of AI labels on reach on any platform.

## 3. Motion design, faceless and animated videos: retention, captions, sound, pacing, safe zones, AI voice

### Takeaway
Evidence here is the weakest in the brief: almost all numbers come from vendors selling caption or faceless-video tools, and they disagree. The defensible points are that captions help, voice quality matters more than whether the voice is AI, and text must stay out of the bottom UI zone.

### Cited Findings
- [ANECDOTE, vendor] Animated word-by-word captions are claimed to lift retention or watch time by 10-34% versus static or none; methods are unpublished and figures differ by vendor — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/caption-styles-drive-views); [BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/animated-captions-vs-static-captions); [ClipSpeed](https://clipspeed.ai/blog/ai-captions-increase-views.html)
- [ANECDOTE, vendor] Sound-off share is quoted anywhere from about 60% to 85% of short-form viewing — [ByteCap](https://www.bytecap.io/blog/science-of-silent-viewing-short-form-video-retention)
- [ANECDOTE, vendor] Pacing rules conflict: change the visual every 2-4 s, with a dip after 5 s on one image (FluxNote, citing an undocumented 5,000-Shorts analysis); every 1.5-3 s (a Reels editor); a real pattern break every 8-12 s that changes attention, not just a cut (GoFaceless) — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/faceless-shorts-retention-tips-2026); [GoFaceless](https://www.gofaceless.ai/en/blog/optimize-video-engagement-pattern-breaks)
- [PRACTITIONER] SFX should land on the exact frame of the cut; Instagram is said to reward subtler sound design than TikTok — [Edición Video Pro](https://edicionvideopro.com/?p=2264)
- [PRACTITIONER, conflicting] Safe zone on a 1080x1920 canvas: about 1080x1420 centred; top reserve about 250 px; bottom reserve quoted as 280, 340, 350 or 450-480 px depending on the guide; right edge about 90 px. No official Meta spec surfaced — [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/); [TryMyPost](https://www.trymypost.com/blog/instagram-reels-safe-zones-text-placement-2026); [BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-size-instagram-reels-2026)
- [DATA STUDY, not short-form] TechSmith: 768 people, same tutorial with only the voice changed; the high-quality AI voice had the best retention apart from one low-quality human outlier. Framing: voice quality matters more than AI versus human — [TechSmith](https://techsmith.com/blog/ai-voices-avatars-in-training-videos)
- [DATA STUDY, older, 2024] Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services: human voice-over in short video ads lowers cognitive load and raises purchase intention versus AI voice-over (purchase intent, not retention) — [RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/joreco/v81y2024ics0969698924003011.html)
- [ANECDOTE, vendor] AI-voiced and human-voiced short videos perform within 5% when niche and hook are controlled (the vendor sells AI voice) — [Kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/ai-voice-vs-human-voiceover-2026/)
- [DATA STUDY] Consumers notice AI voices and trust human voices more (Adobe survey) — [ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/ai-voices-in-marketing-adobe/)

### Inferences
- Checkable items: burned-in captions present; no key text in roughly the bottom 450 px, top 250 px or right 90 px (a conservative synthesis of conflicting guides, not a published spec); no static frame held beyond about 5 seconds; SFX synced to cuts and not overloaded.
- AI voice is not a red flag in itself on current evidence; a flat or low-quality voice is.

### Gaps
- No independent study of animated versus static captions, of caption-style fatigue ("Hormozi style"), of kinetic typography, colour/contrast or minimum text size.
- No editor or creator community reports were reachable.

## 4. Packaging: captions and social SEO, hashtags, audio, timing, Collabs, cross-posting, technical specs

### Takeaway
The hashtag cap is confirmed (Instagram: five since December 2025; TikTok: five rolling out since August 2025, not documented officially), and keywords in captions matter more than hashtags. Watermarks from other apps are the one packaging error with an official reach consequence.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, via press] Instagram capped posts and Reels at five hashtags (down from 30), announced via @creators on 18 Dec 2025; caption and comment hashtags reportedly count together. A cap of three was tested earlier, so articles citing three are outdated — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/); [Creatorflow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/how-many-hashtags-instagram/)
- [DATA STUDY] Metricool 2026: posts with hashtags average 32% fewer views and 34% fewer interactions (correlation, not causation, per Metricool) — [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/etude-instagram-formats-leviers-fonctionnent-2026/)
- [DATA STUDY] Fanpage Karma (1.6M posts): five hashtags gave the highest reach, four close behind; zero hashtags beat two or three — [Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/how-many-hashtags-should-you-use-on-instagram/)
- [PRACTITIONER, unconfirmed officially] TikTok five-hashtag cap rolling out since August 2025, not on every account, not in the Help Center; caption limit quoted as 2,200 or 4,000 characters — [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/tiktok-5-hashtag-limit); [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/tiktok-hashtag-limit); [RecurPost](https://recurpost.com/blog/trending-tiktok-hashtags)
- [DATA STUDY] Natural-language keywords in captions now weigh as much as format choice (Metricool, as relayed) — [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/etude-instagram-formats-leviers-fonctionnent-2026/)
- [OFFICIAL, older, second-hand] Mosseri: Instagram tries not to recommend Reels carrying other apps' logos; your own logo is fine; content edited outside Instagram is not penalised — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/); [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/does-instagram-penalise-tiktok-or-capcut-content/)
- [ANECDOTE] "A watermark costs about 90% of reach" is unverified — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/avoid-content-duplication-penalties-cross-posting-2026)
- [PRACTITIONER] Business accounts are limited to Meta's royalty-free Sound Collection; popular licensed tracks are cleared for personal/creator use only. Account type affects which songs are available, not ranking — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/brands-vs-creators-instagram-rules/); [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/instagram-music-library); [Third Chair](https://usethirdchair.com/blog/instagram-sound-library-rules-for-creators-and-brands)
- [DATA STUDY, older, 2023, second-hand] Fanpage Karma: Reels using songs already in 100,000+ Reels got 17% more reach and 180% more interactions. No rigorous 2026 trending-versus-original audio study found — [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/social-media-terms/trending-audio)
- [DATA STUDY, conflicting] Best time to post: Buffer (9.6M posts) Thursday 9am, Wednesday 12pm and 6pm; Later (6M posts, 2024 data) 5am local, Reels at midnight Monday; Metricool (24.4M posts) Monday 8pm; Siècle Digital relays 7-9pm on any day; Sociality 8pm-midnight — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram); [Later](https://later.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/best-time-to-post-instagram/); [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2026/07/05/cette-etude-revele-ce-qui-fonctionne-reellement-sur-instagram-en-2026/)
- [DATA STUDY] Reel length: Socialinsider (about 6M brand Reels, Jan-Jun 2026) finds 45-60 s has the highest median views (about 10,374) and engagement (about 0.35%); another Socialinsider page says views keep rising with length while under-30 s wins engagement — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)
- [DATA STUDY] Collabs: Emplifi H1 2026 finds creator-published collabs beat standard brand posts and brand-published collabs, with Reels gaining most; a Dash Social sample of 116 campaign posts shows a 67% higher average but no median advantage; an academic study of 100 collab posts finds reach behaves like a weighted average of both accounts, not a sum — [Emplifi](https://emplifi.io/resources/instagram-collaborations-boost-organic-engagement/); [ValueYourNetwork](https://www.valueyournetwork.com/en/instagram-creator-collabs-2026/); [Henry Stewart Talks](https://hstalks.com/article/11245/download/)
- [OFFICIAL, older, Oct 2024] Instagram renders lower-quality video for less-viewed Reels and re-renders higher quality if they pick up; Mosseri said quality is not a major engagement factor — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-downgrades-video-quality-for-less-viewed-clips/731152/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Technical checks: 1080p, 9:16, 30 fps, MP4/AAC; turn off HDR capture (HDR-to-SDR conversion causes washed-out or blurry uploads); enable "Upload at highest quality" and disable Data Saver. No official bitrate found — [Lindsey Gamble](https://lindseygamble.beehiiv.com/p/why-your-instagram-post-might-be-blurry); [Guiding Tech](https://www.guidingtech.com/why-are-my-instagram-reels-blurry/)
- [PRACTITIONER] YouTube Shorts: hashtags 2-3 (vidIQ) or up to 5 (Metricool); loops and accurate, curiosity-led titles recommended; Shorts can run 3 minutes since October 2024 — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/get-more-views-youtube-shorts/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/)

### Inferences
- Hard checks: five hashtags or fewer on Instagram and TikTok; no third-party app watermark; SDR export at 1080x1920; music cleared for the account type.
- Soft checks: target keyword in the first line of the caption and in on-screen text; length matched to content, with 45-60 s as a starting point for brand Reels.
- Posting time should not be a QC criterion: five studies give five different answers.

### Gaps
- Nothing found on cover/thumbnail impact, on-screen titles, pinned comments, replying to comments with video, or broadcast channels.
- Google indexing of captions is asserted by blogs without a study.

## 5. Engagement tactics that work or backfire

### Takeaway
Replying to comments is the only tactic with broad data support. Comment-keyword automation is allowed but sits next to Instagram's engagement-bait line; rage bait raises engagement at a cost to trust.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA STUDY] Buffer: accounts that reply to comments perform better on all six platforms with reply data (including Instagram); lift quoted at 5-42% depending on platform, 42% on Threads — [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/replying-to-comments-boosts-social-media-engagement-across-all-major-platforms-data-shows/)
- [OFFICIAL, older, 2024] Instagram: single-word comment CTAs are fine within guidelines; engagement bait is content whose goal is to inflate counts rather than connect — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/718151/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Misleading keyword bait can be reported, and reports count against the account; a vendor argues reach drops blamed on DM automation usually come from aggressive growth behaviour — [Creatorflow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/manychat-shadowban-risks/)
- [PRACTITIONER] January 2026: comment-trigger automations failed in an incident ManyChat attributed to Meta; DM keyword triggers were the workaround — [InstantDM](https://instantdm.com/blog/manychat-automation-not-working-2026)
- [DATA STUDY] Columbia Business School: courting outrage lifts engagement metrics but backfires on the brand — [Columbia Business School](https://business.columbia.edu/insights/courting-outrage-social-media-backfires)
- [PRACTITIONER] Marketing Brew (Oct 2025) frames rage bait as widespread and contested among marketers — [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2025/10/10/ragebait-is-everywhere-should-marketers-lean-in/)
- [ANECDOTE] "Platforms cut engagement-bait reach by 50-90%" is unsourced — [PostIgniter](https://postigniter.com/blog/engagement-bait-vs-engagement-triggers-what-social-media-algorithms-actually-reward-in-2026)
- [PRACTITIONER, conflicting] Sharing a Reel to Stories: ManyChat says it does not change algorithmic distribution and only reaches existing followers; another blog claims a first-30-minutes boost with no data — [ManyChat](https://manychat.com/blog/how-to-share-a-reel-to-instagram-stories/); [Socialz](https://socialz.ai/blog/how-to-share-a-post-to-instagram-story)
- [OFFICIAL, second-hand] Top ranking signals per Mosseri (January 2025): watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; sends matter slightly more for non-followers. "Sends weigh 3-5x likes" is not attributed to him — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources//instagram-algorithms/); [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm)

### Inferences
- A CTA that asks for a share or a genuine reply is aligned with the stated signals; a CTA that only farms comments is the risk.
- Red flag: a keyword-comment CTA whose promised resource does not match what the DM delivers.

### Gaps
- No data on intentional errors, "wait for it" bait, giveaways, or broadcast-channel pushes.

## 7. Common reasons videos flop, per post-mortems

### Takeaway
Post-mortems converge on four causes: weak opening, payoff that does not match the hook, production friction, and nothing worth sharing. None of this comes from a rigorous study.

### Cited Findings
- [PRACTITIONER] A large drop in the first 1-3 seconds means the hook failed; the opening is "the test", not a branding warm-up — [Contra](https://contra.com/p/QOmWlLms-when-reels-flop-how-to-analyze-and-learn-from-low-performing-videos); [Async](https://async.com/blog/reel-retention-tips/)
- [ANECDOTE] One analysis claims viral videos held about 82% of viewers past 3 seconds versus 41% for flops (unnamed dataset) — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/bulkdl/why-your-video-didnt-go-viral-7-diagnoses-from-analyzing-50000-videos-2026-10o1)
- [PRACTITIONER] An overpromising hook trades a strong first second for a collapsing retention graph — [PodcastVideos](https://www.podcastvideos.com/articles/instagram-reels-retention-strategies-2026)
- [PRACTITIONER] Friction list: hard-to-hear audio, shaky visuals, low-contrast text, dim lighting — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/why-instagram-reels-getting-low-views/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Method: wait 72 hours, read the retention graph, compare with your last five strong Reels, change one variable at a time — [Contra](https://contra.com/p/QOmWlLms-when-reels-flop-how-to-analyze-and-learn-from-low-performing-videos)
- [OFFICIAL] Instagram replaced "view rate" with "skip rate" (share of views swiped away within 3 seconds); retention metrics added November 2025; no official benchmark exists — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/); [TechEDT](https://www.techedt.com/instagram-introduces-retention-metrics-to-help-creators-improve-reels-performance)

### Inferences
- Pre-publication red flags that follow: the first second contains a logo or greeting instead of the subject; the hook promises more than the video delivers; text is low-contrast; no moment a viewer would send to someone.

### Gaps
- Nothing found specifically on "topic too broad", inside jargon, or CTA placed too early.
