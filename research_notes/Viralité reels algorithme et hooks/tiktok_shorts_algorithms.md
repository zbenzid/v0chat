> Researcher note on method and reliability (read first). Research date: 2026-11-13. In this session direct page fetches were blocked for every host tried (newsroom.tiktok.com, npr.org, techcrunch.com, ppc.land, gizmodo.com, buffer.com all failed DNS resolution from the sandbox). All findings below therefore come from web-search result digests of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. URLs are the pages the search engine attributed the facts to. Quotes marked "(as quoted in search digest)" should be re-checked against the page before being reproduced verbatim in a published report. Each finding is tagged: **[OFFICIAL]** (platform statement or platform employee speaking for the platform), **[LEAK]** (internal document, via press or court filing), **[DATA STUDY]** (third-party quantitative analysis), **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** (blogs, agencies, tool vendors, creator lore). ## Key Question 1 — TikTok: official signals, leaked documents, batch testing, Creator Rewards, search/carousels/long video, US ownership and retraining

### Takeaway
TikTok has officially confirmed only a qualitative model: user interactions (watch, finish, like, share, skip, comment, follow, search) weigh most, video information second, device/account settings least, with "finishing a longer video" named as a strong signal and follower count stated not to be a direct factor; the only quasi-quantitative disclosures come from the 2021 NYT "Algo 101" leak (score = predicted like/comment/play x value + expected playtime x value, serving retention and time spent) and from the Creator Rewards Program (1-minute+ original videos, qualified views of 5+ seconds, formula of originality, play duration, search value, audience engagement). The famous "200-500 view batch / traffic pool tiers" numbers and the "70% completion threshold" circulating in 2026 have no TikTok source; and the US joint venture has announced it will retrain the algorithm on US data but has published no ranking changes or timeline.

### Cited Findings

#### 1A. Official statements on ranking signals
- **[OFFICIAL]** (June 18, 2020; page still live, re-dated variants exist incl. a Nov 1, 2023 regional copy) TikTok's newsroom post "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou" groups signals into three families: user interactions, video information, and device/account settings; device and account settings receive the least weight. — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/); [TikTok Newsroom, 2023 regional copy](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tik-tok-recommends-video-for-you?lang=en-MY)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** (2020, restated in 2026 summaries) Interactions listed by TikTok: videos you have watched, finished, liked, shared or skipped; accounts you follow; comments you post; videos you create; later summaries of TikTok's help pages add searches. — [SocialPilot summary of TikTok newsroom/help center, Sept 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm); [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you?lang=en)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** (June 2020) Strong vs weak signals: a full watch ("finishing a longer video") and shares are strong signals; weak signals include device, language preference, and whether viewer and creator are in the same country. — [Tech Brew, June 22, 2020](https://www.techbrew.com/stories/2020/06/22/tiktok-provides-details-recommendation-system); [TechCrunch, June 18, 2020](https://techcrunch.com/2020/06/18/tiktok-explains-how-the-recommendation-system-behind-its-for-you-feed-works)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Follower count is not a direct ranking factor (neither follower count nor prior high-performing videos are direct factors in the recommendation system). — [SocialPilot, Sept 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm); [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** The system "adjust[s] for things you indicate you're not interested in"; "Not interested" tells TikTok to show less content like that post; TikTok says it typically does not recommend content you have already seen and deliberately diversifies feeds so they do not become "a homogeneous stream of videos". — [TikTok Newsroom, 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you?lang=en); [Social Media Today explainer](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/how-tiktoks-algorithm-works-as-explained-by-tiktok/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** User controls that feed the model: "Not interested", "Manage Topics" (sliders for 10+ topic categories inside Content Preferences), and a full For You refresh (resets recommendations to popular content and rebuilds from reactions; cannot be undone). — [SocialPilot, Sept 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Eligibility gate before ranking: videos that violate rules are removed; videos that break no rule but are unsuitable for a broad audience are made ineligible for For You recommendation; only videos passing moderation enter the recommendation pool. — [Relevant Audience summary of TikTok Help Center/Community Guidelines](https://www.relevantaudience.com/tiktok-ads/tiktok-algorithm-for-you-feed/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** (Nov 2025) TikTok added a control letting users choose how much AI-generated content they see in For You and is testing invisible AI watermarking; this is a separate system from originality/eligibility rules. — [Tubefilter, Nov 19, 2025](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/11/19/tiktok-generative-ai-content-toggle-trust-safety/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Fast-skipping (swiping away quickly) is treated as a negative signal. Plausible given "skipped" is on TikTok's official interaction list, but the strength is not officially quantified. — [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/how-the-tiktok-algorithm-works)

#### 1B. Leaked and litigation-disclosed internal documents
- **[LEAK]** (NYT, Dec 5, 2021, document titled "TikTok Algo 101", produced by TikTok's Beijing engineering team; details below via secondary coverage) The document states the company optimizes for two closely related metrics, "retention" (whether a user comes back) and "time spent", in service of growing daily active users. — [Gizmodo, Dec 2021](https://gizmodo.com/leaked-tiktok-doc-reveals-its-obvious-secret-to-an-addi-1848166901); [DeepLearning.AI The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/what-makes-tiktok-tick)
- **[LEAK]** The simplified scoring equation reported from the document: `Plike x Vlike + Pcomment x Vcomment + Eplaytime x Vplaytime + Pplay x Vplay` (predicted probability of a like, comment, and play, each times a value weight, plus expected playtime times its weight). The NYT did not define the terms; The Batch's reading is that it estimates the value of showing a given video to a given user. — [DeepLearning.AI The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/what-makes-tiktok-tick); [Inverse/Input](https://inverse.com/input/culture/tiktok-internal-docs-reveal-just-how-simple-it-is-to-keep-you-addicted)
- **[LEAK]** The document frames four goals: "user value", "long-term user value", "creator value", and "platform value". — [RouteNote summary](https://licensing.routenote.com/blog/tiktok-recommendation-algorithm-explained/)
- **[LEAK]** Per the Times' description, watch (or rewatch) time "isn't the only ingredient"; likes, comments and plays also feed the score. Verification caveat: the Times did not publish the document and Gizmodo could not obtain a copy, so the formula is a reported approximation, not a verified spec. It is also 5 years old in 2026. — [Gizmodo, Dec 2021](https://gizmodo.com/leaked-tiktok-doc-reveals-its-obvious-secret-to-an-addi-1848166901)
- **[LEAK]** (Oct 11, 2024; Kentucky AG complaint whose redactions failed, reported by NPR/Kentucky Public Radio) Internal TikTok research identified the amount of viewing needed to form a habit: 260 videos, after which, per state investigators, a user "is likely to become addicted to the platform". Because videos can be as short as a few seconds, investigators said this can happen in under 35 minutes. — [NPR, Oct 11, 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/10/11/g-s1-27676/tiktok-redacted-documents-in-teen-safety-lawsuit-revealed); [OPB/NPR](https://opb.org/article/2024/10/11/tiktok-knows-its-app-is-harming-kids-new-internal-documents-show)
- **[LEAK]** Same filings: TikTok's internal test of its screen-time limit tool found only about a 1.5-minute drop in daily usage. A follow-up NPR piece (Oct 13, 2024) covers further disclosures, including that the algorithm boosted "attractive" people. — [NPR, Oct 11, 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/10/11/g-s1-27676/tiktok-redacted-documents-in-teen-safety-lawsuit-revealed); [NPR Illinois, Oct 13, 2024](https://www.nprillinois.org/2024-10-13/inside-the-tiktok-documents-stripping-teens-and-boosting-attractive-people)
- **[OFFICIAL]** TikTok's response to the Kentucky disclosures (spokesman Alex Haurek): the complaint "cherry-picks misleading quotes and takes outdated documents out of context." The complaint was sealed by a state judge after publication. — [NPR, Oct 11, 2024](https://www.npr.org/2024/10/11/g-s1-27676/tiktok-redacted-documents-in-teen-safety-lawsuit-revealed)

#### 1C. Batch testing / tiered traffic pools: credible vs myth
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** What is supported: TikTok shows a new video to a small group of users first (interspersed with popular videos) and widens distribution if that group responds positively. TikTok has not published batch sizes, tier counts, or thresholds. — [SocialPilot, Sept 2026](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/tiktok-ads/tiktok-algorithm-for-you-feed/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION — flagged as myth/unverified]** The "first batch of 200-500 viewers, then 1,000-8,000, then ..." tier ladder has no TikTok source. One analysis traces it to Chinese explainers about Douyin's "traffic pool" that were copied into English/Spanish/Portuguese blogs without sourcing; Douyin has officially said only that more traffic means more evaluation and a stricter standard, which is an analogy for TikTok, not evidence. — [cut.pro analysis, 2026](https://cut.pro/en/blog/tiktok-stuck-at-200-views-2026)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION — unverified]** A 2025-2026 "follower-first testing" pattern (new videos seeded to followers before non-followers) is based on creator observation; the blog promoting it concedes TikTok has not announced it. — [Social Boost Digital](https://socialboostdigital.com/blog/tiktok-follower-first-test-2026)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION — unverified]** A "~70% completion rate threshold for virality" in the post-JV US algorithm is repeated on SEO blogs; a fact-check page notes it appears on no TikTok Newsroom, JV, or Oracle disclosure. — [FastClips facts-vs-rumours page](https://fastclips.app/learn/tiktok-us-algorithm-joint-venture)

#### 1D. Creator Rewards Program (CRP)
- **[OFFICIAL]** (March 18, 2024, TikTok Newsroom "Introducing the new Creator Rewards Program") The CRP (renamed from Creativity Program Beta) rewards "high-quality, original content that is over a minute long"; the reward formula is built on four metrics: originality, play duration, search value, and audience engagement. — [TikTok Newsroom, Mar 18, 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Definitions from the launch post: "search value" is a metric assigned to content based on popular search terms (tied to the Creator Search Insights tool); "play duration" covers both watch time and finish rate; the formula rewards content that is clear and engaging "rather than favoring accounts with an excessive amount of videos"; originality means a distinct point of view or creative thought. — [Search Engine Journal, Mar 2024](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/tiktoks-new-creator-rewards-program-prioritizes-search-value/511376/); [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Qualified view definition as relayed by multiple guides: a view from the For You feed, lasting at least 5 seconds, one per account, on an original video of 1 minute or longer; fraudulent, paid/promoted, artificial, "disliked"/"not interested" and repeat views from the same account are excluded. — [Neal Schaffer guide](https://nealschaffer.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [Creators Agency guide, 2026](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026); [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/qualified-views-vs-total-views)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Eligibility: 18+ (19 in South Korea), 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days, Personal account in an eligible country; eligible videos must be posted after joining, be 1 minute+, original (Duets, Stitches and sponsored content excluded), and reach at least 1,000 qualified For You views. — [Neal Schaffer guide](https://nealschaffer.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [Manychat guide](https://manychat.com/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Conflict to note: some guides state a 30-second threshold instead of 5 seconds; one says TikTok's Creator Academy counts Search-originated views when watched 30+ seconds. Could not be reconciled against TikTok's own support page (blocked). — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/qualified-views-vs-total-views); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-creator-next/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Reported RPM ranges: about $0.40-$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (other reports $0.36-$1.04); TikTok publishes no fixed rate. — [Neal Schaffer guide](https://nealschaffer.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/); [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/blog/tiktok-pay-per-view-what-creators-earn-in-2026)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** As of July 20, 2026, US Creator Rewards terms are issued by TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC. — [Social Scale Hub](https://www.socialscalehub.com/academy/tiktok-us-ownership-2026-how-algorithm-changes-impact)

#### 1E. Search / TikTok SEO
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Creator Search Insights is TikTok's built-in tool (under Creator Tools, for eligible creators) showing what users search for, with a "Content Gap" view flagging topics with high search interest and relatively little content; topic pages suggest hashtags and keywords. — [Soup Agency, 2026](https://soupagency.com.au/blog/how-to-use-tiktoks-creator-search-insights-tool-to-leverage-your-tiktok-seo/); [Sirency](https://www.sirency.com/blog/tiktok-seo)

#### 1F. Longer videos and photo carousels
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary — dated 2023]** At an October 2023 creator event (reported by The Information), TikTok said users spend half their time on the app watching videos longer than 60 seconds, and that over the previous six months creators posting 1-minute+ videos gained followers five times faster than those posting shorter videos. — [Tubefilter, Nov 27, 2023](https://www.tubefilter.com/2023/11/27/tiktok-long-form-video-watch-time/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-says-users-spending-half-time-minute-clips/700748/)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Buffer (1.1 million TikToks): videos longer than 1 minute get 63.8% more watch time than 30-60 second videos and more reach; about 86% of TikToks posted are under a minute. Correlational. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/research-tiktok-longer-videos-get-more-reach/742754/)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Carousels, conflicting results. Fanpage Karma (about 700,000 posts, Jan-May 2025): TikTok carousels had about 3% more reach than video and an engagement rate 81% higher, but about one third fewer shares. — [Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Metricool TikTok Study 2026 (2.31 million posts, about 92,000 accounts, Jan-Feb 2025 vs 2026): video generated 5.6x more views and 7.8x more interactions than image/carousel posts (Metricool's own summary page says 5x and 6x); image/carousel engagement rate fell 50%; overall content volume up nearly 80% while per-post performance declined. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-study/); [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/tiktok-content-volume-up-nearly-80-percent-in-early-2026-as-per-post-performance-declined-report-finds/)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Buffer 2026 (45M+ posts across platforms): on TikTok, video has a median engagement rate of 3.39% vs 1.92% for carousels/photo posts (77% higher). — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/)

### Inferences
- For a script checklist, the only signal weightings with documentary support are: (a) completion of the video, especially a longer one (official "strong signal"); (b) shares (official strong signal); (c) expected playtime, likes, comments, plays (leaked formula); (d) absence of skip / "not interested" (official inputs and CRP exclusion). Rewatch is plausible (leak coverage mentions "watch (or rewatch) time") but TikTok has never officially ranked it against other signals.
- The CRP formula is a monetization formula, not the For You ranking formula. It is nonetheless the clearest public statement of what TikTok says it wants: 1-minute+ original videos, high play duration (watch time plus finish rate), search-matched topics, engagement. Treat it as a strong hint of ranking direction, not as proof of ranking weights.
- The 5-second qualified-view rule implies a practical hook requirement: a view that ends before 5 seconds is worth nothing in rewards and almost certainly registers as a skip. Script QC should therefore test the first 5 seconds, not just the first 1-3.
- Because official 2020 language says prior video performance and follower count are not direct factors, each video is effectively judged on its own early audience response; this is consistent with an expanding-audience test but does not validate any specific tier numbers.
- US vs non-US audiences may diverge from 2026 onward if retraining on US-only data proceeds; there is not yet evidence that it has changed script-level best practice.
- Carousel data flipped between 2025 (carousels competitive on reach, better on engagement rate) and early 2026 (video far ahead). The safe reading for a video-script checklist is that video is the primary format in 2026 and carousels are a secondary format whose advantage has eroded.

### Gaps
- The exact wording of TikTok's current official pages (Newsroom, support.tiktok.com "How TikTok recommends content", Creator Academy, Transparency Center) could not be read directly; tiktok.com hosts were unreachable. The official 5-second definition of qualified views and the 30-second claim for search views remain unreconciled.
- The original NYT article (Ben Smith, "How TikTok Reads Your Mind", Dec 5, 2021) was not surfaced or fetched; details such as "ultimate goal of adding daily active users", suppression of repetitive content to avoid boredom, and creator-monetization weighting are from the researcher's memory of that article and were NOT re-verified in this session.
- No official TikTok statement was found on the weight of rewatches, saves/favorites, or comments relative to watch time. All "points per action" tables (e.g., rewatch = 5 points, share = 3) are unsourced practitioner lore and were excluded.
- No official data on what share of TikTok views comes from search, and no 2025-2026 official statement on photo-carousel ranking, were found.
- No primary document on how (or whether) the US algorithm has actually been retrained as of November 2026.
- Other 2024 lawsuit disclosures (multi-state AG suits beyond Kentucky) were not examined in detail.

## Key Question 2 — YouTube Shorts: what YouTube officials have said, view counting, length, myths, exploration, originality

### Takeaway
YouTube's official line (Todd Sherman, Creator Insider, YouTube Help) is that Shorts ranking is driven by audience response in a swipe feed, where the key observable is whether people choose to watch or swipe away, followed by how much they watch, rather than click-through rate; thresholds are deliberately not published, posting time and upload volume are officially non-factors, and delete-and-repost is discouraged as spam-like. Three dated mechanics changes matter for 2025-2026: Shorts up to 3 minutes (Oct 15, 2024), public "views" counting every play or replay with no minimum watch time while "engaged views" keeps the old definition for monetization (Mar 31, 2025), and a ranking change favoring original Shorts over re-uploads (Oct 1, 2026).

### Cited Findings

#### 2A. Official statements on how Shorts ranking works
- **[OFFICIAL]** (Aug 2023, Creator Insider Q&A with Todd Sherman, then Shorts product lead; video id n3jsYK_-aRU) The core framing: "The audience is the algorithm" (as quoted in search digest); distribution follows viewer response. — [Creator Insider on X, Aug 23, 2023](https://x.com/YouTubeInsider/status/1694453024528425357); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Shorts vs long-form: in long-form, viewers choose by clicking a title/thumbnail, so CTR matters; in Shorts viewers swipe through a feed, so YouTube looks at whether they watch versus swipe away. Sherman called "viewed vs swiped away" a great metric for creators to watch in Analytics. YouTube has not said this exact number is the ranking signal. — [TechCrunch, Aug 25, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/); [Buffer summary](https://buffer.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Thresholds (what counts as a view, what rate is "good") are intentionally not shared to prevent gaming. In 2023 Sherman said not every flip in the Shorts feed counted as a view, unlike platforms where the first frame counts (this view definition changed in March 2025, see 2B). — [TechCrunch, Aug 25, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/); [Creator Handbook](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/youtube-explains-how-the-youtube-shorts-algorithm-works/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Exploration / seed audience: Sherman explained the "spike then flatline" pattern. A Short gets an initial push of a few hundred or thousand views as the system tries to find an audience; these early views are exploratory, and distribution continues or drops depending on how those viewers respond. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Length: Sherman advised prioritizing the story over a target duration; on recommendations, the system tries to judge whether a viewer really enjoyed a video regardless of its length. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/); [Piktochart, 2026](https://piktochart.com/blog/how-long-youtube-shorts/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Rene Ritchie (Creator Liaison) framing: the algorithm "pulls" videos for viewers rather than "pushing" them for creators. — [Elite Content Marketer, 2026](https://www.elitecontentmarketer.com/get-more-youtube-views-for-free/)

#### 2B. March 31, 2025: views vs engaged views
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Announced March 26, 2025, effective March 31, 2025: a Shorts "view" now counts every time a Short starts to play or replays, with no minimum watch time. Previously a view required a minimum number of seconds watched. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/); [Digital Music News, Mar 27, 2025](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/03/27/how-does-youtube-shorts-count-views-2025/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** The previous definition survives as "engaged views" (viewer continued watching beyond the initial start), visible in YouTube Analytics Advanced Mode. YPP eligibility and Shorts ad revenue sharing continue to rely on engaged views, so the change does not affect earnings. YouTube advises using engaged views to compare content published before and after March 31. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-view-count-update/); [Sprout Social help](https://support.sproutsocial.com/hc/en-us/articles/35874991211533-YouTube-Shorts-View-Count-Update-March-2025); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/youtube-shorts-will-see-more-view-counts-earnings/489190)

#### 2C. Three-minute Shorts
- **[OFFICIAL]** YouTube Help ("Understand three-minute YouTube Shorts"): videos uploaded after October 15, 2024 with a square or vertical aspect ratio and up to three minutes long are categorized as Shorts. A later entry on the page lists a December 8, 2025 date with the same criteria (meaning not determined). — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en-EN)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** YouTube's stated rationale: more room for storytelling. — [Piktochart, 2026](https://piktochart.com/blog/how-long-youtube-shorts/); [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/youtube-shorts-is-making-a-huge-change-to-its-maximum-video-length-and-it-will-affect-creators/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Three minutes is a ceiling, not a target; guides note that evidence favoring 50-60 second Shorts over very short clips predates the change. — [Descript](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be); [OpenClip](https://openclip.app/guides/youtube-shorts-length)

#### 2D. Officially debunked myths
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Posting time: Shorts does not prioritize specific times of day; timeliness matters only for news-type topics. YouTube's FAQ says publish time "is not known to impact a video's long-term performance". — [Benzinga, Aug 2023](https://www.benzinga.com/news/23/08/34020532/youtube-shorts-chief-debunks-popular-myths-no-posting-10-videos-a-day-doesnt-affect-your-performance); [The Leap, 2025](https://www.theleap.co/blog/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Volume: there is no magic number of Shorts per day; posting 10 a day does not by itself change performance; Sherman urged quality over quantity. — [Benzinga, Aug 2023](https://www.benzinga.com/news/23/08/34020532/youtube-shorts-chief-debunks-popular-myths-no-posting-10-videos-a-day-doesnt-affect-your-performance)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Delete and repost: Sherman recommended against deleting and reposting a Short to make it take off because of the risk it is seen as spam. The YouTube Blog's "Rene's Top Five" (Apr 18, 2024) confirms Sherman addressed hashtags and deleting/reposting in a follow-up episode. — [TechCrunch, Aug 25, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/); [YouTube Blog, Apr 18, 2024](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/renes-top-five-on-youtube-april-18-2024-edition/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Scheduling delay: Rene Ritchie said (reported Nov 2025) that keeping an upload private/unlisted for 24-48 hours before publishing offers no strategic performance advantage. — [My Internet Marketing Tools, Nov 2025](https://myinternetmarketingtools.com/youtubes-rene-ritchie-dispels-upload-delay-myth-for-creators/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION — quote unverified]** "Shorts will not hurt your long-form content" attributed to Ritchie/Sherman on a management-agency blog with vague sourcing. — [Market Maker Mgmt](https://marketmakermgmt.com/blog-list2/do-youtube-shorts-hurt-your-channel)

#### 2E. 2026 changes
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** October 1, 2026: YouTube adjusted Shorts recommendation systems to rank original Shorts higher and reduce reach of re-uploads that add nothing new, per a TeamYouTube Help Community post quoted by press. Target: channels that mainly aggregate or re-upload others' videos without significant changes; judged at channel level; not a ban on clipping (commentary, narration, substantial editing can count as original); applies to the Shorts feed, with monetization still governed by the reused-content policy. YouTube disclosed no percentage decrease, detection threshold, or penalty duration; channels that shift to original work are re-evaluated. — [MediaPost, Oct 5, 2026](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/418466/youtube-alters-shorts-algorithm-for-originality.html); [Vanguard, Oct 2026](https://www.vanguardngr.com/2026/10/youtube-changes-shorts-algorithm-to-favour-original-videos/); [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/youtube/youtube-shorts-original-content-reach-update/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION — single source, unverified]** The dislike button was removed from the Shorts player on June 25, 2026, leaving "Not interested" and "Don't recommend this channel" as the negative-feedback controls. — [OutlierKit update tracker](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-algorithm-updates/); [AITuber tracker](https://aituber.app/blog/youtube-algorithm-changes/)

#### 2F. Data-study benchmarks for "viewed vs swiped away"
- **[DATA STUDY]** (April 14, 2023) Paddy Galloway with analyst Chris Gileta: 3.3 billion views, 5,400 Shorts, 33 channels. Shorts with under 60% "viewed vs swiped away" rarely performed well; the best performers were typically between 70% and 90%. Galloway's takeaway: make the first second punchy, "treat your intro like a thumbnail"; engagement metrics (likes/comments) "don't seem massively important". He called it early research. — [Paddy Galloway on X](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898356419981315); [Takeaway #2 post](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898373365051392?lang=en); [Thread Reader copy](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1646898356419981315.html)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Same study: most Shorts in the sample were 20-40 seconds; very short Shorts were rare. — [Paddy Galloway on X](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898359808974851)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Community-observed bands for viewed-vs-swiped: 70-79% "working", 80%+ "strong"; the same source notes YouTube publishes no official benchmark. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** A vidIQ guide's claimed rough order of Shorts signal weight: viewed vs swiped away, then average percentage viewed including rewatches, then shares and substantive comments. Not official; another guide stresses YouTube has published no signals list or weights for Shorts. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)

### Inferences
- The two-stage structure implied by official statements is: (1) the swipe decision in roughly the first second or two (viewed vs swiped away), then (2) how much of the Short is watched and whether the viewer was satisfied. A script checklist should therefore separate "opening frame/first line" checks from "body retention" checks.
- Since March 31, 2025, public Shorts view counts are inflated relative to earlier periods and include instant swipes and loops; "views" is no longer a quality signal. Any benchmark quoting views per Short from before April 2025 is not comparable; engaged views is the consistent series.
- The Galloway 70-90% benchmark is from April 2023 and predates both 3-minute Shorts and the view-count change. The viewed-vs-swiped metric itself is a ratio in Analytics and was not redefined by the March 2025 public-view change as far as found, so the benchmark is probably still directionally usable, but this is an inference, not a confirmed fact.
- The October 2026 originality change raises the cost of cross-posted or clipped material on Shorts at channel level, which matters for any workflow that republishes the same vertical video across three platforms from an aggregator-style channel; first-party original videos are not the stated target.
- Official silence on likes/comments plus Galloway's finding that engagement metrics "don't seem massively important" suggests script effort on Shorts is better spent on hook and retention than on comment-bait.

### Gaps
- The Creator Insider video and YouTube Help pages could not be opened; exact Sherman quotes on hashtags, thumbnails, and what counts as a view are not verified here. From the researcher's memory (not re-verified): Sherman said hashtags are not required and their usefulness varies, that custom thumbnails matter little for Shorts because most views come from the feed, and that Shorts and long-form recommendations are largely separated. Treat as unverified.
- No official statement was found that explicitly confirms loops/rewatches as a positive ranking signal; this is practitioner inference from average-percentage-viewed exceeding 100%.
- No official 2025-2026 statement on Shorts "freshness" (how long a Short can keep being recommended) was found; practitioner claims that Shorts can resurface months later were not sourced.
- A claim that public view counts change again on "August 24" appears in one research blog ([CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/research/youtube-view-count-change/)); year, scope (Shorts vs long-form) and official source could not be verified.
- A Todd Beaupré 2025 remark that YouTube "weights watch time by satisfaction signals like surveys, returns, and session continuation" is attributed by a vidIQ guide only; no primary transcript found. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/understanding-youtube-algorithm/)
- No large 2025-2026 data study with a median viewed-vs-swiped figure (vidIQ, OpusClip, Retention Rabbit) was found; the only primary dataset remains Galloway 2023.

## Key Question 3 — Cross-platform: common signals, how "satisfaction" is operationalized, watermarks and cross-posting

### Takeaway
All three feeds officially converge on the same core: how long people watch (watch time/completion), whether they skip or swipe away, and active responses, with shares/sends the most consistently emphasized positive action and "not interested"-type feedback the explicit negative; "satisfaction" is operationalized through surveys (YouTube's 1-5 star "valued watchtime"), explicit negative feedback, and exclusions of disliked views (TikTok CRP). On watermarks, Instagram is the only platform with an explicit, on-the-record recommendation penalty for other apps' logos; TikTok treats unedited reused/watermarked content as ineligible for For You; YouTube's documented rule concerns monetization and, since October 2026, channel-level down-ranking of re-uploads, not a specific watermark detector.

### Cited Findings

#### 3A. Signals common to Reels, TikTok and Shorts
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Instagram (Adam Mosseri, January 2025): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends"; creators should track average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach. He described sends as slightly more important than likes for reaching non-followers (unconnected reach). — [Social Media Today, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION — unverified]** Claims that sends are weighted "3-5x" likes on Instagram have no primary source. — [Virlo](https://virlo.ai/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026-decoded)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** TikTok: full watch (finishing a longer video) and shares are strong signals; skips and "not interested" are inputs. — [Tech Brew, June 2020](https://www.techbrew.com/stories/2020/06/22/tiktok-provides-details-recommendation-system)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** YouTube Shorts: watch-vs-swipe is the Shorts analogue of CTR; the system tries to assess whether the viewer really enjoyed the video. — [TechCrunch, Aug 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/25/youtube-demystifies-the-shorts-algorithm-views-and-answers-other-creator-questions/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Follower/subscriber count is not a direct factor on TikTok; all three platforms test content on an initial audience and expand based on response (TikTok small group; Shorts exploratory early views). — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/tiktok-algorithm); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-explains-how-shorts-algorithm-works/494953/)

#### 3B. How "satisfaction" is operationalized
- **[OFFICIAL]** YouTube Blog, "On YouTube's recommendation system" (2021, still the reference text): YouTube measures "valued watchtime", time spent on videos the viewer finds worthwhile, through surveys asking viewers to rate a watched video from one to five stars; the system combines clicks, watchtime, survey responses, sharing, likes, and dislikes. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/on-youtubes-recommendation-system/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Only watch time on videos rated four or five stars counts as "valued watchtime" (repeated in third-party guides; the search digest could not confirm this cutoff in the YouTube blog excerpt). — [Search Engine Journal, 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-youtubes-recommendation-system-works-in-2025/538379/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** YouTube's named feedback signals include watch history, search history, subscriptions, likes, dislikes, "Not interested", "Don't recommend channel", and satisfaction surveys. — [Search Engine Journal, 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-youtubes-recommendation-system-works-in-2025/538379/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** TikTok: "Not interested" reduces similar content; in the Creator Rewards Program, views marked not interested/disliked do not count as qualified views, a direct economic penalty for regretted views. — [Creators Agency](https://creatorsagency.co/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026); [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/)
- **[LEAK]** TikTok's internal objective per Algo 101 is retention (return visits) and time spent, i.e., long-term value is defined behaviorally, not by survey. — [Gizmodo, Dec 2021](https://gizmodo.com/leaked-tiktok-doc-reveals-its-obvious-secret-to-an-addi-1848166901)

#### 3C. Watermarks and cross-posting, platform by platform
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Instagram (Feb 2021, still in force): content "visibly recycled from other apps" (e.g., with TikTok watermarks) is made less discoverable in Reels/Explore; it still reaches followers. — [Social Media Today, 2021](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-now-limit-the-reach-of-re-posts-from-tiktok-within-its-reels/594803)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Mosseri clarification: Instagram tries not to recommend Reels with logos from other apps, but a creator's own logo/branding is fine. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-that-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** Instagram originality policy: "If most of what you post to Instagram is someone else's content, your account is no longer going to be recommendable" (Mosseri, as quoted in search digest); protections extended to photos reuploaded with a different watermark or caption. — [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy); [Digital Camera World](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/two-years-later-instagram-is-finally-giving-photographers-the-same-protection-as-videographers-with-this-key-change)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** TikTok: reused content with no creative edits, including clips carrying another account's watermark, is ineligible for the For You feed. — [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/tiktok-ads/tiktok-algorithm-for-you-feed/)
- **[OFFICIAL]** YouTube Shorts monetization policies: non-original Shorts are ineligible for revenue, e.g., unedited clips from others' movies/TV, reuploads of other creators' content from YouTube or another platform, compilations with no original content added. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary — dated 2021]** For the original Shorts Fund, YouTube said uploads could not carry watermarks from other apps (TikTok, Reels, Snapchat), but the same video re-uploaded without the watermark could qualify, i.e., cross-posting your own content was not excluded. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/future-of-tv/youtubes-creator-fund-for-youtube-shorts-will-not-exclude-videos-posted-to-other-platforms)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** YouTube adds its own watermark to Shorts downloaded from Studio so cross-posted copies point back to YouTube. — [Social Media Today](https://socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-will-now-automatically-add-watermarks-to-downloaded-shorts-clips/629940/)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION — unverified]** Claims that YouTube "actively suppresses" watermarked Shorts in recommendations, or that CapCut/Reels watermarks trigger automatic TikTok flags, come from marketing blogs without platform documentation. — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/avoid-content-duplication-penalties-cross-posting-2026)

### Inferences
- Common denominator for a cross-platform script checklist: (1) stop the swipe in the first 1-3 seconds; (2) hold attention to the end (completion / average percentage viewed); (3) earn a share/send (content worth passing to a specific person); (4) avoid regret (misleading hooks that produce "not interested", low survey ratings, or early exits). Likes and comments are secondary on every platform's stated ordering.
- Satisfaction systems punish hooks that over-promise: a bait hook raises viewed-vs-swiped but lowers completion and survey/negative-feedback outcomes. The checklist should require that the payoff matches the hook.
- Cross-posting the same original video is tolerated everywhere; what is penalized is (a) visible third-party watermarks (explicit on Instagram, eligibility issue on TikTok, monetization/originality issue on YouTube) and (b) channel-level patterns of posting others' content. Export clean masters with no platform logos.

### Gaps
- No official TikTok or Instagram documentation of in-app satisfaction surveys feeding short-video ranking was retrieved in this session (both platforms are known to run "are you interested in this" style prompts, but no citable source was found here).
- No official YouTube statement that surveys are applied specifically to Shorts ranking, or how often, was found.
- No official YouTube statement confirming or denying a recommendation-level watermark penalty for Shorts.
- "Regretted views" as a named metric: no platform source found; the concept appears only in practitioner and academic commentary not retrieved here.
- Instagram Reels specifics were only touched for cross-platform comparison; another researcher is presumed to cover Reels in depth.

## Key Question 4 — Quantitative benchmarks: retention, hook rates, length ranges (2025-2026)

### Takeaway
No platform publishes an official "good" retention number; the benchmarks that exist are vendor or creator datasets of uneven quality. The most defensible reference points are: viewed-vs-swiped-away of 70-90% for strong Shorts with under 60% rarely working (Galloway 2023); average percentage viewed around 73% on Shorts, about 78% on TikTok and about 65% on Reels (Socialinsider 2025, via secondary); a 3-second hold around 50-60% average with 60-70% for top performers (unpublished methodology); and a length split where 15-30 seconds wins on engagement rate while 45-60 seconds and 1-minute+ win on views and watch time.

### Cited Findings

#### 4A. Hook / early retention
- **[DATA STUDY]** Shorts viewed-vs-swiped-away: under 60% rarely performs; best performers 70-90% (3.3B views, 5,400 Shorts, 33 channels; April 2023). — [Paddy Galloway on X](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898356419981315); [Thread Reader](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1646898356419981315.html)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Community bands for Shorts viewed-vs-swiped: 70-79% workable, 80%+ strong. — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/)
- **[DATA STUDY — low transparency]** An analysis of 50,000+ short-form video assets puts average 3-second hold at 50-58%, top 20% at 60-70%, and describes 60%+ as the threshold for secondary distribution; methodology unpublished. — [Grow-it retention benchmarks](https://www.grow-it.se/en/resources/short-form-video-retention-benchmarks); [Mean CEO statistics roundup](https://blog.mean.ceo/video-hook-retention-statistics/)
- **[DATA STUDY — old, undated Meta research]** 65% of viewers who watch the first 3 seconds watch at least 10 seconds, and 45% watch 30 seconds or more. — [Kapwing statistics roundup, 2026](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/short-form-video-statistics-tiktok-reels-and-shorts-by-the-numbers-in-2026/)
- **[OFFICIAL, via secondary]** TikTok's monetization floor for a counted view is 5 seconds (qualified view). — [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program/)

#### 4B. Average percentage viewed / completion
- **[DATA STUDY, via secondary]** Socialinsider (Aug 2025 report as relayed): average view completion about 73% on YouTube Shorts, about 78% on TikTok, about 65% on Reels. — [Virvid](https://virvid.ai/blog/ai-shorts-increase-retention-watch-time); [GoFaceless roundup](https://www.gofaceless.ai/en/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate-benchmarks)
- **[DATA STUDY, via secondary]** Shorts average retention about 73.6% per vidIQ (June 2026) and 73% per ShortsIntel (2026); viral Shorts (1M+ views) average about 76% retention per a 2025 study. — [GoFaceless roundup](https://www.gofaceless.ai/en/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate-benchmarks)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Length-adjusted Shorts retention targets: above 60% for sub-15-second, above 50% for 15-30 seconds, above 40% for 30-60 seconds. — [GoFaceless roundup](https://www.gofaceless.ai/en/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate-benchmarks)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Shorts completion rate above about 30% is solid and 50%+ strong. — [Grow Creator](https://growcreator.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-retention-rate-benchmark)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Average percentage viewed can exceed 100% when viewers loop; a 15-second Short can reach 300%+; looping is described as one of the strongest positive signals (not officially confirmed). — [1of10](https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-shorts-analytics/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)

#### 4C. Length ranges
- **[DATA STUDY, via secondary]** Socialinsider (nearly 6 million TikToks): highest engagement rate at 15-30 seconds (about 6%), falling to 4.2% at 30-60 seconds. — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Buffer (1.1 million TikToks): videos over 1 minute get 63.8% more watch time than 30-60 second videos and higher reach; 86% of TikToks are under 1 minute. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/)
- **[DATA STUDY, via secondary]** Socialinsider 2026: Reels of 45-60 seconds get almost double the median views of Reels under 30 seconds or over 3 minutes. — [Joyspace data roundup](https://joyspace.ai/ideal-video-length-social-platform-2026)
- **[DATA STUDY, via secondary]** A study of 35 billion Shorts views found a bimodal optimum: about 13 seconds or a full 60 seconds. — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Galloway 2023: most Shorts creators publish 20-40 second Shorts. — [Paddy Galloway on X](https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1646898359808974851)
- **[PRACTITIONER OPINION]** Suggested ranges: 15-30 seconds on TikTok/Reels and 20-35 seconds on Shorts for awareness content; 35-60 seconds for denser content. — [ByteCap](https://www.bytecap.io/blog/optimal-short-form-video-length-retention-analysis)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Hard platform constraints: TikTok Creator Rewards requires 1 minute+; YouTube Shorts allows up to 3 minutes for uploads after Oct 15, 2024. — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-new-creator-rewards-program?lang=en); [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en-EN)

#### 4D. Context benchmarks (engagement, 2026)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Socialinsider 2026 TikTok benchmarks: average engagement rate about 3.85% in H1 2026 (reported as an 8% quarterly drop on one page; other Socialinsider pages cite 4.20-4.30% stable from Q1 2025 to Q1 2026); average views per post 6,496 (+3%). Internal inconsistency across pages noted. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-engagement-rate-study/)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Dash Social 2026: TikTok engagement dipped from 3.9% to 3.4% (different methodology); brands average about eight posts per week; brands posting fewer than six times a week see 93% higher engagement. — [Dash Social](https://www.dashsocial.com/social-media-benchmarks/tiktok)
- **[DATA STUDY]** Metricool 2026: TikTok content volume up nearly 80% year over year in early 2026 while per-post performance declined. — [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/tiktok-content-volume-up-nearly-80-percent-in-early-2026-as-per-post-performance-declined-report-finds/)

### Inferences
- Usable QC targets for a script, with honesty about their softness: design the opening so that 70%+ of feed viewers stay past the first second or two (Shorts), aim for 60%+ still watching at 3 seconds, and aim for average percentage viewed at or above roughly 70-80% for sub-30-second pieces, with lower absolute percentages acceptable for 45-90 second pieces as long as absolute watch time is high.
- Length is a trade-off, not a single optimum: shorter scripts maximize percentage-based metrics (completion, loops, engagement rate), longer scripts maximize absolute watch time and, on TikTok, monetization eligibility (1 minute+). A checklist should ask "which metric is this video built for?" rather than enforce one duration.
- The bimodal Shorts result (about 13 seconds or about 60 seconds) and TikTok's 15-30 second engagement peak suggest the weakest zone is a mid-length video that is too long to loop and too short to deliver depth; scripts in the 30-45 second range need a stronger retention structure.
- Rising content volume with falling per-post performance in 2026 implies benchmarks from 2023-2024 overstate what an average post now gets; compare against the account's own trailing median rather than industry averages.
- Loop-friendly endings (last line flowing into first line) are the only way to push average percentage viewed above 100%; valuable on Shorts and short TikToks, but no platform officially confirms a ranking bonus for loops.

### Gaps
- No official platform benchmark exists for 3-second hold, completion rate, or viewed-vs-swiped; every number above is third-party.
- The primary Socialinsider, vidIQ, ShortsIntel and "35 billion Shorts views" reports were not opened; figures are relayed through aggregator blogs and their sample definitions (completion vs average percentage viewed) may differ.
- The 50,000-asset 3-second-hold analysis has no published methodology and its attribution between the two cited pages is uncertain; treat as directional only.
- No 2025-2026 study was found that measures TikTok hook rate (percentage past 2 or 3 seconds) on a large organic sample; TikTok Analytics exposes this per video but no credible aggregate was located.
- No post-March-2025 replication of Galloway's viewed-vs-swiped benchmark was found.
- OpusClip and Retention Rabbit datasets named in the brief did not surface with primary figures.
